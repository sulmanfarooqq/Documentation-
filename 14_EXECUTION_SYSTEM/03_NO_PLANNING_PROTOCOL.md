# No-Planning Protocol
Planning stops when the next action is clear; research must lead to contact or delivery.
Do not create another niche list, service, logo, dashboard, or tool stack during the 14-day test.
Research only enough to verify a buyer, public signal, and relevant workflow problem.
Limit custom demos to a small reusable example; never deliver unpaid production work.
If blocked for 30 minutes, ask the responsible teammate or reduce the task to a testable step.
Change strategy only from recorded evidence: reply rates, call conversion, deposits, and delivery results.
Review plans weekly; execute daily.
