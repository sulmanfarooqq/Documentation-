# MARKETING — PROOF, NOT PERFORMANCE

Three engineers create enough technical material naturally.

The marketing job is to turn real work into trust.

## Ownership

A: commercial narrative and distribution.
B: technical demo/architecture evidence.
C: editing, case-study structure, QA of claims.

## Weekly

- 2 professional posts
- 1 short technical/business demo
- 1 portfolio/proof improvement

## Content hierarchy

1. verified client result
2. real before/after workflow
3. real demo
4. technical lesson tied to a business problem
5. generic educational content

Never invent:
- revenue
- lead percentages
- client logos
- testimonials
- case studies
- performance claims

Content should support sales, not replace it.
