# Lean Marketing
Use one public demo showing a lead move from intake to routing, follow-up, and failure alert.
Explain the workflow and its limits; do not claim fake clients, results, or guaranteed revenue.
Publish one short teardown or demo weekly only after daily sales actions are complete.
Every post supports the same buyer and offer, with one clear call to discuss the workflow.
Ask permission before using client names, screenshots, data, or outcomes.
Measure qualified inquiries, not likes; stop content tasks that displace outreach.
