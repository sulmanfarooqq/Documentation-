# CONTENT BLUEPRINT

Content is evidence, not entertainment.

## Three formats

### 1. Teardown

Show a real business workflow and explain:
- current friction
- automation opportunity
- architecture
- expected operational effect

### 2. Build demo

Show a small working system:
lead -> qualification -> CRM -> notification -> follow-up.

### 3. Lesson from delivery

After a real project:
- what was broken
- what changed
- what failed
- what you learned
- verified result

## Weekly minimum

- 2 useful LinkedIn posts
- 1 short demo/visual
- 1 portfolio/proof improvement

Do not create content before completing the daily prospecting quota.

Never publish private client data without permission.
Never manufacture case studies.
