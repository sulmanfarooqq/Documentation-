# Reality Check
Three engineers without paying customers are not yet a validated agency; they are an unvalidated team.
More services, niches, and documentation will not replace buyer conversations.
Sell one narrow workflow fix to buyers with visible evidence of need.
Do not assume a demo, website, or technical skill creates trust by itself.
Collect a deposit, deliver measurable acceptance criteria, and earn permission to show proof.
Review cash and conversion weekly; do not count unpaid interest as revenue.
Expansion waits until one offer sells and delivers repeatedly.
