# NICHE SYSTEMS — REPLACEMENT FOR THE 50-NICHE LIST

The old 50-niche list creates the illusion of preparation. It encourages jumping between markets.

## Pick one buyer for 14 days

Choose the group where you can identify the problem quickly and reach decision-makers.

### Strong starting signals

**Agencies**
- repetitive lead handling
- client onboarding
- reporting
- CRM updates
- proposal/admin workflows

**Real estate**
- lead routing
- qualification
- appointment booking
- follow-up

**Home/service businesses**
- missed calls
- quote requests
- booking
- review follow-up

**E-commerce**
- support questions
- order-status automation
- lead/customer routing

**Professional services**
- intake
- document routing
- reporting
- CRM hygiene

## Offer ladder

1. Workflow audit/demo — free or low-friction
2. Small automation sprint — $150–$500
3. Multi-workflow implementation — $500–$1,000+
4. Ongoing optimization/support — priced after proof

## Do not promise

- guaranteed revenue
- guaranteed percentage improvements
- fully autonomous behavior without controls
- compliance/security claims you cannot substantiate

The niche is a targeting mechanism, not your identity. Your underlying capability is business-process automation.
