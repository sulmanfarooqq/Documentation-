# Daily Execution Dashboard
**Single goal:** win a paid, tightly scoped lead-follow-up project; do not build speculative features.
A / Revenue: 10 researched first contacts, 10 follow-ups, update every lead stage, request deposits.
B / Delivery: prove feasibility, document one milestone, flag blockers before they threaten scope.
C / QA + Proof: define acceptance tests, test edge cases, record demo evidence, prepare handoff.
Track: new contacts, positive replies, calls, proposals, deposits, cash collected, QA pass rate.
No evidence or buyer pain = no outreach; no deposit = no custom implementation.
40 qualified contacts with zero positive replies: change signal, buyer, or message—not just volume.
Daily review: what shipped, what sold, blocker, owner, next action and deadline.
