## RULES

- Do not change niche because one day was quiet.
- Do not change the offer without evidence.
- Do not buy another tool because outreach feels uncomfortable.
- Do not count "research" as outreach.
- Do not count sent messages as sales.
- Do not count revenue until money is actually received.

## END-OF-DAY QUESTIONS

1. What did I actually ship?
2. How many real buyers did I contact?
3. Where did prospects stop responding?
4. What objection appeared repeatedly?
5. What will I change tomorrow?

Run this for 14 days before making major strategic changes, unless the offer is clearly impossible, unethical, technically infeasible, or contradicted by direct market evidence.
