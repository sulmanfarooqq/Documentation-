# Read This Before You Plan Again
Your bottleneck is not another framework; it is verified buyer conversations and paid delivery.
For 14 days, sell one lead-follow-up fix to real-estate agencies with observable workflow gaps.
Do not rotate niches, redesign the brand, add services, or build a product during the test.
Every workday: 10 qualified first contacts, 10 follow-ups, and a recorded next action per lead.
Build only after scope, acceptance criteria, access, and the upfront payment are confirmed.
If the numbers fail, diagnose the funnel stage; do not hide behind more documentation.
A document has value only when it changes today's action or prevents a delivery failure.
