# SIGNAL-FIRST HUNTING
- Find accounts showing **why now**: sales/operations hiring, new locations, lead-generation activity, CRM migration, or explicit automation request.
- Verify one observable clue on a public page/post/job listing; save its URL and date.
- Score: +3 explicit demand, +2 timely trigger, +2 visible workflow gap, +1 reachable buyer, −3 no evidence.
- Contact only scores ≥5; prioritize explicit demand over speculative “AI opportunity.”
- Identify owner/operations/sales lead; do not message random staff or scrape/blast lists.
- Message: “I noticed [specific evidence]. If [workflow] is still manual, we can map a small fix: [outcome]. Worth a 10-minute check?”
- Send 10 genuinely researched first contacts/day; follow up after 2–3 business days with new value.
- Use email/LinkedIn first; phone/WhatsApp only when public and appropriate; respect opt-outs and platform rules.
- Log evidence, buyer, message, reply, next action, and loss reason in one shared board.
- Never claim this method is unique or guaranteed; its advantage is evidence-backed relevance.
