# ZERO-DOLLAR OPERATING STACK

Free tiers change. Verify current limits before depending on a tool.

## Minimum stack

**Lead research:** LinkedIn + Google search + public company websites.

**Pipeline:** Google Sheets or a free CRM.

**Communication:** business email + Google Meet.

**Documents:** Google Docs/Drive.

**Code:** GitHub.

**Demo hosting:** Netlify or another free hosting tier where appropriate.

**Automation:** n8n/Make/Zapier or custom code, selected per client requirement and cost.

**Project tracking:** a simple Kanban board.

**AI:** use the model/tools already available to you; do not buy multiple subscriptions before revenue.

## Pipeline columns

Lead -> Contacted -> Replied -> Qualified -> Call Booked -> Proposal -> Won -> Onboarding -> Delivery -> QA -> Handoff -> Follow-up

## Tool rule

Tools do not create demand.

If a tool requires money and does not directly support a sold project, delay the purchase.

Do not build your agency around a specific vendor's free tier. Build around portable processes.
