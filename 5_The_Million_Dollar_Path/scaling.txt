# Scale Only What Works
Ignore million-dollar fantasies until the first offer sells repeatedly at a healthy margin.
First prove one buyer segment, one offer, reliable delivery checklist, and collected cash.
Track acquisition effort, close rate, delivery hours, defects, gross margin, and repeat demand.
Standardize recurring work before hiring or adding fixed costs.
Raise prices only with stronger proof, clearer value, or higher delivery complexity.
Expand to a second niche only after the first has repeatable sales and delivery.
Scale systems customers pay for; kill vanity work.
