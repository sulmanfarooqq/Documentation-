# SCALING — THREE ENGINEERS

Do not confuse team size with business maturity.

Three engineers can support more delivery capacity than one person, but the company still needs demand.

## Stage 0 — Prove demand

Goal:
- first paid projects
- one repeatable offer
- one reliable sales channel
- proof

Team:
A = revenue + solutions
B = delivery
C = product/QA

Everyone can help. One owner per function.

## Stage 1 — Repeat

Once an offer sells repeatedly:
- standardize discovery
- standardize proposal
- standardize onboarding
- standardize build
- standardize QA
- standardize handoff
- collect proof

## Stage 2 — Increase capacity

Only after sustained sold work:
- delegate repeatable implementation,
- automate internal operations,
- add contractors/specialists,
- protect A's sales time.

## Stage 3 — Build a company

Possible structure:
- commercial/client function
- delivery function
- technical/product function
- project/account coordination
- specialist bench

Do not build this org chart before the revenue requires it.

## Hiring trigger

Hire when:
1. work is already sold,
2. bottleneck is identified,
3. task is repeatable,
4. Definition of Done exists,
5. QA exists,
6. unit economics support the hire.

Never hire to feel bigger.

## Revenue reality

A target such as "$10k/month" is a planning target, not evidence.

Track:
cash collected, gross margin, delivery hours, sales activity, pipeline conversion, repeat business, and client concentration.

Growth that creates chaos is not operational maturity.
