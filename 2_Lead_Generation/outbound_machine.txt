# THREE-PERSON OUTBOUND MACHINE

## One commercial owner

A owns outbound.

B and C contribute technical observations, demos, and niche research only when needed.

This prevents the classic team failure:

"Everyone is responsible for sales" = nobody is responsible.

## 14-day test

Choose:
- one buyer type,
- one painful workflow,
- one offer,
- one primary channel.

Do not run five niches because three people are available.

## Daily A quota

- 20 qualified prospects researched
- 10 personalized first contacts
- 10 follow-ups
- 2 direct asks for a short call
- all activity logged

B/C spend at most 20–30 minutes helping A with technical personalization when useful.

## Prospect signal

Look for:
- manual lead handling
- WhatsApp-heavy sales
- repetitive support
- spreadsheets between systems
- disconnected CRM
- slow response
- repeated data entry
- booking/admin bottlenecks
- visible software/process gaps

## Message

Observation -> operational problem -> relevant outcome -> low-friction CTA.

Do not lead with "we are a team of AI experts."

## Pipeline quality

Track:
Lead / Contacted / Replied / Qualified / Call / Proposal / Won / Lost / Nurture.

A owns the truth of this board.

## Hard rule

More engineers do not justify more outreach complexity.

The commercial system stays simple until the market gives evidence for expansion.
