# OUTBOUND MACHINE — STAGE 1

Do not wait for inbound.

## One target at a time

For a 14-day sprint, choose:
- one buyer type
- one painful workflow
- one offer
- one primary channel

Example offer:
"We automate your new-lead flow so inquiries are captured, qualified, routed and followed up without manual copying."

## Daily numbers

Minimum:
- 30 qualified prospects researched
- 20 personalized first contacts
- 10 follow-ups
- 2 direct asks for a short call
- every response logged

Quality matters, but "personalized" does not mean writing a 500-word essay for every prospect.

## Prospect selection

Look for evidence:
- contact forms
- slow/manual booking
- WhatsApp-heavy sales
- multiple spreadsheets
- disconnected CRM/tools
- repetitive customer questions
- obvious lead-response gaps

## Outreach formula

Observation -> business problem -> relevant outcome -> low-friction CTA.

Do not claim savings or results you have not measured.

## Follow-up

Follow up several times with useful context, not "just checking in".

Stop when they decline, ask you to stop, or the sequence reaches its limit.

Outbound is a process. Random messages are not a process.
