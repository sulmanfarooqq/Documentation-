# Outbound Operating Rules
Build a small verified list; each lead needs a signal, source URL, buyer, and next action.
Send 10 personalized first contacts and 10 follow-ups each workday.
Lead with the observed workflow issue and ask a low-friction question; avoid generic AI pitches.
Follow up once after 3–4 business days; stop after a clear no or opt-out.
Record every response and loss reason; never invent personalization or prior contact.
After 40 qualified contacts with no positive replies, change the message, buyer, or signal.
Do not scale volume until replies and delivery capacity justify it.
