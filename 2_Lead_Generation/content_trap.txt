# CONTENT IS NOT THE PIPELINE

Three engineers can easily turn content into a comfortable substitute for selling.

Do not.

Until the agency has repeatable demand:
1. outbound and referrals come first,
2. sales conversations come second,
3. delivery/proof comes third,
4. content documents the proof.

## Team ownership

A owns the commercial calendar.
C turns real delivery lessons into proof assets.
B supplies technical demonstrations and architecture evidence.

Nobody spends half a day making a "viral" post while qualified prospects are untouched.

## Weekly minimum

- 2 useful LinkedIn/professional posts
- 1 short build/demo/proof asset
- 1 portfolio or case-study improvement when real evidence exists

Content must come from:
- a real problem,
- a real build,
- a real lesson,
- a real verified result.

Never manufacture case studies, metrics, testimonials, or client logos.

## Priority rule

If pipeline activity is incomplete, content gets shortened.

A sales message sent to a real buyer is more commercially useful than another generic AI post.
