# Content Is Not the Sales Pipeline
Content is secondary until direct outreach proves the offer can attract buyer conversations.
Publish one useful workflow teardown or short demo only when it supports the current offer.
Every claim must be accurate, specific, and backed by evidence or labeled as a hypothesis.
Do not polish posts while qualified leads receive no follow-up.
Measure qualified conversations and attributable opportunities, not views alone.
Reuse one piece across channels; stop formats that consume time without useful signals.
