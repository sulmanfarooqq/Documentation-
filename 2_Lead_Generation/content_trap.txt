# CONTENT: SUPPORT SALES, DO NOT REPLACE SALES

Content is secondary until Flow Vello has paying customers.

## Priority order

1. Direct outreach
2. Follow-up
3. Discovery calls
4. Delivery and proof
5. Case studies
6. Content distribution

Do not spend three hours making generic "Top 5 AI trends" posts.

## Useful content

Publish proof-oriented material:
- teardown of a real workflow problem
- short before/after automation
- architecture diagram
- demo of a small automation
- lesson from a real client delivery
- quantified result when the number is verified

Never invent client results. If it is a demo, label it as a demo.

## Minimum cadence

2 useful posts/week is enough during Stage 1.

Content must support an offer you are actively selling. It is not a substitute for prospecting.
