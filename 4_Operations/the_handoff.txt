# CLIENT -> DELIVERY HANDOFF

The project does not enter development until the scope is clear.

## Required handoff

1. Business problem
2. Desired outcome
3. Current workflow
4. Future workflow
5. Systems involved
6. Required access
7. Exact deliverables
8. Explicit exclusions
9. Definition of Done
10. Deadline
11. Client approver
12. Risks/unknowns

## Rule

Client language must be translated into testable work.

"Build an AI agent" is not a specification.

"Agent receives website inquiry, extracts name/service/date, checks approved knowledge, asks one missing question, creates CRM lead, and escalates uncertain cases to a human" is a specification.

If the requirement cannot be tested, it is not finished.
