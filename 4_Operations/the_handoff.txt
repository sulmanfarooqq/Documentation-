# Delivery Handoff
B documents workflow map, integrations, configuration, deployment, and recovery steps.
C attaches acceptance-test results, edge cases, known limitations, and unresolved defects.
A confirms client training, written acceptance, remaining payment, and support boundaries.
Transfer ownership and access securely; remove team access when no longer needed.
Record only client-approved facts that may be reused as proof.
A project is not complete until the client can operate it and the agreed payment is settled.
