# CLIENT MANAGEMENT — THREE-PERSON TEAM

Every client has one accountable owner: A.

B owns delivery execution.
C owns QA/release evidence.

The client should not have to ask three engineers for status.

## Single source of truth

Track:
- scope
- milestones
- owner
- deadline
- status
- blockers
- approvals
- access
- decisions
- links
- change requests

## Communication

A sends the client-facing status update.

Internal format:
**Done / Next / Blocked / Risk / Decision needed**

## Escalation

- Technical blocker -> B
- Quality/release blocker -> C
- Scope/payment/client decision -> A

Escalate within the same working day.

## Rule

The client experiences Flow Vello as one company, not three freelancers in a group chat.
