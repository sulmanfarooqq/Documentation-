# Client Management
One named client approver; all requirements and decisions are written in one place.
A owns communication, expectation-setting, change requests, and acceptance.
Weekly update: completed work, evidence, next milestone, risk, and decision needed.
Scope changes require revised price, timeline, and written approval before work.
Protect client data; use least-privilege access and approved credential sharing.
Escalate delays and failures early; never conceal defects or promise impossible dates.
Close with acceptance, final payment, documentation, access transfer, and feedback permission.
