# Daily Team Workflow
Start: A updates lead pipeline; B picks one priority delivery milestone; C defines today's test/proof task.
Resolve blockers directly; do not wait for the next meeting.
Before build, confirm written scope, payment, access, and acceptance tests.
Before release, C signs off tests and B verifies deployment and rollback.
A confirms client acceptance, outstanding payment, and next business opportunity.
End: record evidence, numbers, blockers, and next actions in the existing dashboard.
