# Three Role Scorecards
A — Revenue Lead: 10 qualified first contacts, 10 follow-ups, calls, proposals, deposits, client decisions.
B — Delivery Lead: feasibility, architecture, integrations, deployment, technical notes, risk escalation.
C — QA + Proof Lead: acceptance tests, edge cases, release checks, evidence, handoff, permission-based case study.
A owns the client promise; B owns implementation; C owns release quality.
No shared ownership without one named directly responsible person.
Measure outcomes weekly; redistribute workload only when evidence shows a bottleneck.
The same person may help another role, but accountability remains singular.
