# ROLE SCORECARDS

## A — Revenue & Solutions
Own:
- qualified pipeline
- outbound
- follow-ups
- discovery
- proposals
- commercial scope
- client communication

Daily minimum:
- 10 personalized first contacts
- 10 follow-ups
- pipeline update
- active client decisions answered

Success evidence:
- conversations
- qualified opportunities
- proposals
- deposits
- repeat/expanded clients

Failure pattern:
Coding all day while the pipeline is empty.

## B — Delivery & Automation
Own:
- implementation
- automation
- integrations
- deployment
- technical milestones

Daily minimum:
- one meaningful committed milestone
- blockers escalated same day

Success evidence:
- shipped milestones
- stable deployments
- predictable delivery
- low rework

Failure pattern:
Building outside scope or polishing architecture without business need.

## C — Product, QA & Systems
Own:
- acceptance criteria
- QA
- release checks
- product/full-stack work
- proof
- internal systems

Daily minimum:
- one QA/product/proof milestone
- critical defects surfaced early

Success evidence:
- QA-approved releases
- fewer escaped defects
- useful demos
- reusable SOPs

Failure pattern:
Passive reviewing or building internal tools nobody needs.

## Cross-team rule
Each person may help another seat. The seat owner still owns the result.
