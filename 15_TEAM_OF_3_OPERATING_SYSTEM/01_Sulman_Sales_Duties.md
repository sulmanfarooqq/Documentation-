# GOD LEVEL TEAM DISTRIBUTION

If you want to grow, you must use ruthless silos. Nobody overlaps. If you overlap, you will fail.

## Person 1: The Frontline Hunter (Sulman)
1. You do not touch code, you do not manage projects, you only generate cash.
2. You send 30 personalized Loom videos via Apollo to local business owners every day.
3. You handle all objections, close the deal, and send the Stripe payment link.
4. If the agency has zero clients, it is 100% your fault and you are failing the team.
5. Your job ends the exact second the money hits the bank account.

## Person 2: The Logic Engineer (Backend)
1. You do not talk to clients, and you do not care about how the website looks.
2. You take the client's FAQ data and build the conversational AI logic inside Voiceflow.
3. You build the Make.com webhook to route captured leads into the client's calendar.
4. You aggressively test the bot to ensure it never hallucinates or breaks the flow.
5. You hand the final 1-line Javascript snippet to Person 3 and move on.

## Person 3: The Integration Sniper (Frontend)
1. You do not sell, and you do not build AI logic.
2. You log into the client's WordPress or Shopify site and inject the Javascript snippet.
3. You ruthlessly test the widget on mobile and desktop to ensure it doesn't break their UI.
4. You tweak the CSS so the chatbot colors match the client's branding perfectly.
5. You send the final working link back to Person 1 to deliver to the client.


--- 3-MAN TEAM DISTRIBUTION ---
PERSON 1 (Sulman - The Pitch): Record the Loom showing the custom mockup and custom bot.
PERSON 2 (Frontend - The Bait): Build a custom, beautiful 1-page mockup of the target's website.
PERSON 3 (Backend - The Hook): Build a custom, working Voiceflow bot trained on the target's business.
ACCOUNTABILITY: You are an Assembly Line. No one waits. Everyone hunts.
