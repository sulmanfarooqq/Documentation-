## Positioning

Flow Vello is a business automation and software partner focused on making operations faster, more connected and easier to manage.

## Core services

### 01 — Workflow Automation
Connect tools and automate multi-step business processes.

### 02 — WhatsApp & Messaging Automation
Capture inquiries, send updates, qualify leads and route conversations.

### 03 — AI Customer Support
Automate common customer questions with controlled escalation to humans.

### 04 — Custom AI Agents
Build agents that use approved business data/tools to perform defined operational tasks.

### 05 — Sales Automation
Automate lead capture, qualification, follow-up and CRM updates.

### 06 — Executive Dashboards
Surface useful operational metrics in one place.

### 07 — Custom Software Development
Build internal tools and software when existing platforms cannot solve the workflow cleanly.

## Commercial model

- Fixed-scope implementation

- Larger custom systems
- Ongoing support/optimization after proof
