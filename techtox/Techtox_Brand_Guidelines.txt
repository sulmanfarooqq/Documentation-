# TECHTOX BRAND GUIDELINES

## 1. CORE IDENTITY
- **Company Name:** Techtox
- **Tagline:** One Unified Growth Engine.
- **Positioning:** An elite, full-service digital agency bridging the gap between custom software, creative branding, and autonomous AI operations.

## 2. COLOR PALETTE
You have three colors. Stop overcomplicating it.
- **Techtox Red:** #FF2E3E (Used for accents, buttons, hovers, and the logo mark).
- **Pure Black:** #000000 (Used for backgrounds, footer, and deep contrast elements).
- **Pure White:** #FFFFFF (Used for typography on dark backgrounds and card backgrounds).

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY
- **Display Font:** Teko (Used for massive, brutalist headers).
- **Body Font:** Rubik (Used for highly legible body copy and UI elements).

## 4. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
- Brutalist, high-contrast, minimalist.
- Generous padding and spacing.
- No rounded, soft, friendly elements. Techtox is a lethal, enterprise-grade weapon.
