# DEVELOPER HANDOFF SOP

Use this when a contractor is involved.

## 1. Business objective

What business problem is being solved?

## 2. User/system flow

Describe the exact trigger -> processing -> action -> exception path.

## 3. Deliverables

Checklist of concrete outputs.

## 4. Technical context

Approved stack, APIs, repositories, environments and constraints.

## 5. Inputs

What data enters the system? Example payloads where possible.

## 6. Outputs

What should be created, updated, sent or displayed?

## 7. Edge cases

What happens when data is missing, an API fails, a duplicate arrives, or an AI response is uncertain?

## 8. Definition of Done

Specific tests that must pass.

## 9. Deadline

Internal deadline must be earlier than the client commitment.

## 10. Handoff package

Repository, setup instructions, environment variables template, test cases, known limitations and rollback instructions.

## Contractor rule

Never give a contractor vague authority over the whole client relationship.

They receive a scoped task. You own the client promise and final QA.
