# Client Intake — Complete Before Build
Business, decision-maker, timezone, billing details, and day-to-day workflow owner.
Current lead source, destination CRM/sheet, tools, permissions, and existing process.
Show one real example from lead arrival through assignment and follow-up; redact sensitive data.
Define failure cases, response rules, opt-outs, data retention, and who may access records.
Agree scope, exclusions, delivery date, acceptance tests, price, and 50% upfront payment.
Client supplies lawful access and test data; never send passwords in plain text.
Kickoff is complete only when payment, access, scope, and acceptance criteria are confirmed.
