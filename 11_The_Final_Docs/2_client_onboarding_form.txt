# CLIENT ONBOARDING FORM

Project starts only after payment, signed scope/approval, and required access are available.

## Business

1. Company name
2. Primary contact
3. Decision-maker/approver
4. Business objective
5. Current process
6. What is failing or taking too much time?
7. What does success look like?
8. Deadline and reason for deadline

## Systems

9. Tools involved
10. Required integrations
11. Admin/API access available?
12. Test/sandbox environment available?
13. Data sources and formats

## Delivery

14. Exact deliverables
15. Explicit exclusions
16. Who will test/approve?
17. What should happen when automation fails?
18. Preferred communication channel

## Security

19. What data is sensitive?
20. What access can be limited to least privilege?
21. Who can revoke access after delivery?

Never ask a client to send secrets in plain chat. Use the client's approved secure method for credentials.
