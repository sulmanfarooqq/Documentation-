# COLD OUTREACH SCRIPT

Use this as a structure, not a spam template.

## Subject

Quick idea for [Company]

## Message

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific observation about their workflow].

That usually creates [specific operational problem] when [context].

I build small automation systems that can [specific outcome: capture/routing/follow-up/reporting] without replacing the tools you already use.

I made a short example for [Company/niche]. Want me to send it?

— Sulman
Flow Vello

## If they say yes

Send the demo, then ask:

"Does this match the problem, or is the bigger bottleneck somewhere else?"

Do not fabricate "we helped X clients" if you have not.

The message should prove that you looked at their business. Mass personalization with fake observations is still spam.
