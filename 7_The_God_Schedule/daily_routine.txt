# DAILY OPERATING SCHEDULE — ZERO TO FIRST $1,000

This schedule exists to stop consumption from replacing execution.

## 08:00–08:30 — Pipeline review

Check:
- new replies
- follow-ups due
- calls
- active delivery blockers

## 08:30–11:00 — Prospecting + outreach

- research 30 qualified prospects
- send 20 personalized messages
- send 10 follow-ups
- log every action

## 11:00–12:00 — Sales/proof

- improve one demo
- prepare discovery questions
- record a short teardown
- send proposals to qualified prospects

## 12:00–13:00 — Break

## 13:00–16:00 — Delivery

If no client work exists:
build one reusable demo directly tied to the current offer.

## 16:00–17:00 — Calls + follow-ups

Do not schedule calls during your main outbound block unless necessary.

## 17:00–17:30 — CRM/admin

Update pipeline and next action for every active prospect.

## 17:30 — Stop

Do not turn "more research" into another form of procrastination.
