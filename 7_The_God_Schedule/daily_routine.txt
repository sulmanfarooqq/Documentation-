# Workday That Produces Evidence
Block 1: verify 10 qualified leads and send personalized first contacts.
Block 2: complete 10 follow-ups and respond to active prospects.
Block 3: qualify calls, send proposals, and request deposits where fit is confirmed.
Block 4: B ships one approved delivery milestone; C tests or prepares proof.
Block 5: resolve one blocker and update the dashboard with evidence and next actions.
Protect sleep and breaks; an unsustainable schedule damages sales and delivery quality.
Do not substitute schedule optimization for actual outreach or shipped work.
