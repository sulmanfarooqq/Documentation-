# FLOW VELLO — MASTER AGENCY OPERATING IDENTITY

## Positioning

Flow Vello helps businesses eliminate repetitive work, connect disconnected systems and build smarter operations through workflow automation, AI agents and custom software.

The services directory is organized around:

1. Workflow Automation
2. WhatsApp & Messaging Automation
3. AI Customer Support
4. Custom AI Agents
5. Sales Automation
6. Executive Dashboards
7. Custom Software Development

## What Flow Vello sells

Not technology.

Flow Vello sells a better business process.

Examples:
- lead captured -> qualified -> routed -> followed up
- customer question -> AI response -> human escalation
- WhatsApp inquiry -> qualification -> CRM -> appointment
- disconnected systems -> synchronized workflow
- scattered metrics -> one executive dashboard
- manual internal process -> controlled automation

## Stage 1 business model

Sell fixed-scope implementation projects.

Start at $150–$500 for a small, clearly bounded problem.
Move toward $500–$1,000+ after successful proof and repeatable delivery.

Retainers come after the client has a working system worth maintaining.

## Founder role

At zero revenue:
- prospect
- sell
- scope
- architect
- deliver
- QA
- document

As revenue grows:
- delegate repeatable implementation
- delegate coordination
- keep ownership of positioning, sales, quality and key relationships

## Agency rule

Do not present seven services to every prospect.

Lead with the one service/problem that matches their visible bottleneck. The rest of the service directory is expansion capacity, not the opening pitch.
