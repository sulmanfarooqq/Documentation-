# Close Through Clarity
Ask the buyer to show the current workflow, failure point, business impact, and desired result.
Confirm urgency, decision-maker, tools, permissions, budget, and approval process.
Propose one workflow with explicit inclusions, exclusions, timeline, price, and acceptance tests.
Ask for 50% upfront; remaining payment is due at agreed acceptance.
Handle objections by clarifying risk, scope, timing, or trust—not by inventing guarantees.
If the buyer cannot commit, agree a dated next step or close the lead as unqualified.
No signed scope and deposit means no custom production work.
