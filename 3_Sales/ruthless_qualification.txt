# Qualify Before You Propose
Pain: what specifically fails, how often, and what does it cost in time or missed follow-up?
Urgency: why solve it now rather than later?
Authority: who owns the workflow and approves spending?
Feasibility: which systems, access, data, and permissions are available?
Budget: can the buyer pay for a scoped fix?
Success: what observable test proves the workflow works?
If pain, authority, feasibility, or budget is missing, do not force a proposal.
