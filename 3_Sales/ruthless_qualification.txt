# QUALIFICATION

Being "ruthless" does not mean insulting prospects or rejecting anyone who cannot pay a huge retainer.

It means protecting your limited time.

## Qualify for

- painful problem
- clear owner
- reachable decision-maker
- urgency
- access to required systems
- realistic scope
- budget appropriate to the project

## Disqualify or pause when

- they want unlimited work
- nobody owns approvals
- requirements change every conversation
- access cannot be provided
- the deadline is impossible
- they expect free production work
- they refuse clear payment terms

## Important

Do not invent an ROI number.

If the prospect says a manual process costs 20 hours/month, document that as their estimate. Validate it during discovery or delivery when possible.

Your first $1,000 can come from several small projects. You do not need five giant clients immediately.
