# RUTHLESS QUALIFICATION — TEAM VERSION

A owns qualification.

A deal is worth engineering time only when there is evidence of:

- real business pain
- accessible decision maker
- clear workflow
- technical access/feasibility
- meaningful urgency
- acceptable scope
- workable payment terms

## Pause/disqualify

Pause when:
- the buyer wants unlimited free work,
- nobody can approve,
- requirements change every conversation,
- required access cannot be provided,
- deadline is technically impossible,
- payment terms are unacceptable,
- the problem is merely "nice to have."

## Team protection

Never let three engineers spend 20 hours discovering that a project was never commercially qualified.

A owns the gate.

B/C can reject technical impossibility.

No one overrides the gate because a project sounds exciting.
