# Daily Hunt Board
Record: company, URL, signal/date, buyer, channel, message, stage, last touch, next action.
Score +3 explicit demand, +2 recent trigger, +2 visible workflow gap, +1 reachable buyer; −3 no evidence.
Contact only credible-fit leads; personalize the first sentence to the verified signal.
Daily target: 10 new qualified contacts and 10 due follow-ups.
Track positive replies, qualified calls, proposals, deposits, and reasons lost.
At 40 qualified contacts with no positive replies, revise buyer/signal/message before scaling.
Never fabricate personal details, pain, referrals, or prior contact.
