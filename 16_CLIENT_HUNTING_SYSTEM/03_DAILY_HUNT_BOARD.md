## Kill rule

If a prospect has:

- no verified need,
- no trigger,
- no reachable buyer,

remove it.

Do not keep dead leads because the spreadsheet looks impressive.

## Research time limit

Maximum initial research:

**5 minutes per account.**

Only spend more time after the account replies or becomes clearly qualified.

## Success metric

The primary metric is not:

**messages sent.**

It is:

**qualified conversations per 100 researched accounts.**

Then:

**proposals per qualified conversation.**

Then:

**cash collected per proposal.**

This prevents the team from optimizing vanity metrics.
