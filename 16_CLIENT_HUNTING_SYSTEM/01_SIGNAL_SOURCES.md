# SIGNAL SOURCES — WHERE TO HUNT

## Priority order

### 1. Explicit project demand

Best source because the buyer has already stated a requirement.

Search for:

- "automation"
- "AI agent"
- "CRM integration"
- "WhatsApp"
- "chatbot"
- "dashboard"
- "API integration"
- "internal tool"
- "workflow automation"
- "WordPress"
- "WooCommerce"
- "custom software"

Use:

- Upwork
- LinkedIn jobs/posts
- Contra
- company project/RFP pages
- founder communities
- relevant professional communities

**Action:** respond to the existing request instead of creating a new need.

---

## 2. Hiring signals

Search company career pages and job databases.

High-value roles:

- RevOps
- Sales Operations
- Operations Manager
- Customer Support
- SDR/BDR
- Marketing Operations
- Automation
- AI
- CRM
- Integration
- Full-stack engineer

Interpret the signal:

| Hiring signal | Possible operational need |
|---|---|
| RevOps | CRM/process complexity |
| SDR/BDR | more lead volume |
| Customer Support | support workload |
| Operations | manual processes |
| Automation/AI | direct automation demand |
| Integration Engineer | systems need connecting |
| Multiple sales hires | lead routing/follow-up pressure |

Never assume the interpretation is true. Verify another signal.

---

## 3. LinkedIn trigger hunting

Look for posts containing:

- "we're hiring"
- "looking for"
- "need help"
- "seeking"
- "launching"
- "expanding"
- "new office"
- "new market"
- "new product"
- "our team is growing"
- "CRM"
- "automation"
- "WhatsApp"
- "AI"
- "workflow"

Founder/operator posts are especially valuable because they often expose the actual problem.

---

## 4. Company website inspection

For local and SMB targets:

Inspect:

- contact form
- booking
- lead capture
- WhatsApp
- chat
- ecommerce
- support
- forms
- CRM references
- integrations
- response path

Example:

If a real-estate company receives property inquiries through several channels but the process appears manual, the opportunity may be lead routing + follow-up + CRM automation.

Do not claim they are losing money unless there is evidence.

---

## 5. Technology signals

For software companies and technical buyers:

Look for:

- outdated stack
- disconnected systems
- missing integrations
- duplicated workflows
- hiring for technology we can implement
- migration/replatforming
- new product launch
- API/integration work

A tech-stack observation is a conversation starter, not proof of a budget.

---

# SIGNAL STACK EXAMPLES

### Real estate

1. Hiring sales agents
2. High inquiry volume visible
3. Multiple inquiry channels
4. CRM or property platform present

Possible offer:

**Lead Response + CRM Automation**

---

### Dental / medical

1. Multiple locations or high appointment volume
2. Booking process
3. Support/admin hiring
4. Repetitive inquiry flow

Possible offer:

**Appointment + Follow-up Automation**

---

### B2B SaaS

1. New sales/marketing hiring
2. CRM exists
3. Integration/API work visible
4. Growth/launch signal

Possible offer:

**Sales/CRM Workflow Automation**

---

### Ecommerce

1. Growing product catalog
2. Customer-support hiring
3. Repetitive order questions
4. WhatsApp/social selling

Possible offer:

**Customer Support + Order Automation**

---

# NEVER DO THIS

Do not search:

> "1000 real estate companies USA"

and immediately email all 1000.

Instead search:

> "real estate companies hiring sales + automation signal"

Then verify the actual company.

The list should be **small enough to understand**.

---

# SOURCE RULE

A source is valuable only if it helps answer:

**"Why should we contact this company today?"**

If it cannot answer that question, it is background data, not a buying signal.
