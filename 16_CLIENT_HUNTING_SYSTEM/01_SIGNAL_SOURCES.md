# Buyer Signals
Prioritize public evidence of active lead generation, CRM migration, missed follow-up, or operations hiring.
Check company sites, job posts, public updates, and first-party posts before contacting.
Save the exact URL, date observed, signal, likely workflow, and decision-maker.
A generic company directory listing is not a buying signal.
Do not claim a company has missed leads unless evidence supports it.
Respect platform rules, privacy laws, opt-outs, and reasonable contact frequency.
No credible signal means skip the lead.
