# 7-DAY CLIENT SPRINT

## Objective

Get the first genuinely qualified sales conversations from zero without mass-spam or speculative development.

## Day 1 — Pick the battlefield

Choose:

- one buyer
- one workflow
- one offer
- one geographic/market segment
- one primary acquisition lane

Example:

**US real-estate teams + lead response + explicit/hiring signals**

Do not change niche for seven days.

---

## Day 2 — Build the trigger list

A:

- 30 trigger accounts
- score each
- identify buyer

B:

- validate the technical pattern

C:

- verify evidence
- prepare one relevant proof/demo

Output:

**30 researched → 15+ qualified**

---

## Day 3 — First wave

A sends:

- 10 highly personalized contacts

B/C:

- support only the strongest opportunities

Do not build anything unless a prospect's situation justifies it.

---

## Day 4 — Second wave

A:

- 10 new contacts
- 10 follow-ups

Review response quality.

If nobody replies, inspect the signal quality before increasing volume.

---

## Day 5 — Conversations

A runs discovery.

B joins technical conversations only when technical feasibility matters.

C records:

- objections
- requirements
- proof gaps

---

## Day 6 — Proposals

Only qualified prospects receive proposals.

Proposal structure:

1. observed problem
2. current workflow
3. proposed change
4. exact scope
5. timeline
6. price
7. acceptance/payment terms

No giant agency deck.

---

## Day 7 — Evidence review

Calculate:

- accounts reviewed
- qualified accounts
- contacts
- replies
- qualified calls
- proposals
- wins
- cash
- hours spent researching
- strongest signal
- weakest signal
- strongest objection

Then change the system.

Do not restart from zero.

---

# 7-DAY FAILURE DIAGNOSIS

### 0 replies

Likely:
- bad list
- weak signal
- wrong buyer
- weak message

Not automatically "need more volume."

### Replies but no qualified calls

Likely:
- weak problem/offer match
- wrong buyer
- vague CTA

### Calls but no proposals

Likely:
- qualification failure
- no real pain
- no urgency
- budget mismatch

### Proposals but no deposits

Likely:
- weak offer
- low trust
- poor scope
- wrong pricing
- no decision process

### Deposits but delivery problems

Sales succeeded.

Operations failed.

Fix delivery rather than changing acquisition.
