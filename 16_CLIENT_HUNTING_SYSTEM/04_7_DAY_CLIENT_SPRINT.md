# Seven-Day Client Sprint
Day 1: finalize one offer and one small generic demo; define scope and exclusions.
Days 2–7: identify and contact 10 qualified buyers daily; complete 10 follow-ups.
Use public evidence and reach the owner, operations lead, or sales lead—not random staff.
Qualify urgency, workflow, systems, decision process, access, and budget before proposing.
Send written scope, timeline, acceptance tests, price, and 50% deposit request.
No deposit means no custom production build; follow up once, then record outcome.
At day 7, diagnose conversion by stage and change one variable based on data.
