# CLIENT HUNTING SYSTEM — SIGNAL FIRST, NOT HOPE FIRST

## Purpose

This system exists to solve one problem:

> **Find companies that have evidence of a problem right now, then contact the person who can buy the fix.**

Flow Vello should not spend hours convincing random companies that automation, AI, websites, or dashboards are useful.

We hunt for **existing demand**.

The three-person team is not going to send thousands of generic messages. We will build a smaller list of accounts with visible buying/work signals, verify the problem, and contact them while the signal is fresh.

---

# 1. THE NEW RULE

### Do not ask:

> "Who might need our services?"

Ask:

> **"Which company has publicly shown that it needs this work now?"**

That changes prospecting from guessing to evidence.

A company becomes a target only when at least one **Need Signal** exists.

A high-priority target has **two or more independent signals**.

Examples:

- A company is actively hiring for a workflow that we can automate.
- A company posted a project/request for the exact type of work.
- A business has a visible broken/manual process.
- A company just launched, expanded, raised money, or opened a new market and now has an operational bottleneck.
- A company is publicly asking for a developer, automation specialist, chatbot, CRM, dashboard, WordPress, or integration.
- A company has a technology/process mismatch that we can verify.
- A company is visibly receiving leads but handling them manually or slowly.

Intent and trigger data are useful because fit alone tells us who could buy; triggers help identify who may buy now. # 2. THE FIVE HUNTING LANES

Do not use every lane every day. Start with Lane 1 and Lane 2.

## Lane 1 — Active work requests

**Highest priority.**

Find people already asking for:

- automation
- AI agents
- CRM integration
- WhatsApp automation
- chatbot
- dashboard
- custom software
- WordPress/WooCommerce work
- API integrations
- internal tools
- workflow automation

Sources:

- Upwork
- LinkedIn jobs/posts
- Contra
- freelance marketplaces
- company career pages
- public RFP/project pages
- founder posts

Why this lane is powerful:

The buyer has already admitted the problem exists.

Do not convince them that the problem matters. Show that Flow Vello can solve it.

---

## Lane 2 — Hiring signals

A company hiring for a role can reveal an operational problem.

Examples:

- hiring RevOps → systems/process complexity
- hiring sales development → lead volume is increasing
- hiring customer support → support workload is increasing
- hiring operations → manual processes may be growing
- hiring automation/AI → internal automation demand exists
- hiring developers for integrations → technical workload exists

Hiring is a trigger, not proof by itself. Combine it with another signal whenever possible.

Apollo's current guidance explicitly treats hiring, funding, leadership changes, technology changes, and intent as useful outbound triggers. citeturn0search6turn0search7

## Lane 3 — Visible operational problems

Search companies manually and inspect:

- contact/lead forms
- booking flow
- WhatsApp handling
- customer support
- CRM
- ecommerce checkout
- website performance
- repeated manual forms
- spreadsheet-heavy operations
- disconnected systems
- broken integrations
- missing automation

The rule:

**Do not manufacture a problem.**

If we cannot point to the evidence, we do not pitch it.

---

## Lane 4 — Growth/event triggers

Look for:

- funding
- new office
- new market
- new product
- acquisition
- rapid hiring
- major partnership
- new sales team
- expansion announcement

These events create new operational load.

But:

**A growth event alone is not enough.**

Find the workflow that the event likely created or amplified.

---

## Lane 5 — Warm network / referrals

Search:

- former classmates
- developers
- founders
- recruiters
- agency owners
- local business contacts
- existing professional connections
- previous leads
- people who know business owners

Ask for a specific introduction, not:

> "Do you know anyone who needs software?"

Instead:

> "Do you know a real-estate team currently handling property inquiries manually through WhatsApp/CRM?"

Specific problems produce better introductions.

# 3. SIGNAL STACK

Score every account before outreach.

### +3 — Direct demand

They explicitly requested the service/project.

### +2 — Strong trigger

They are hiring for a relevant role, expanding, launching, or publicly discussing the problem.

### +2 — Visible operational problem

We can demonstrate the problem ourselves.

### +1 — Strong ICP fit

Right industry, company size, geography, buyer, and economics.

### +1 — Reachable decision-maker

We can identify the founder, owner, COO, Head of Sales, Operations lead, etc.

### -3 — No evidence

Only our imagination says they need it.

### -3 — No budget/scale fit

The company cannot reasonably buy the offer.

### -2 — Wrong buyer

We cannot identify someone who owns the problem.

### Target classes

**8–10 = HOT**
- contact immediately
- personalized research
- use two channels when appropriate

**5–7 = WARM**
- contact after stronger research
- monitor for another signal

**0–4 = COLD**
- do not spend deep research time
- do not build a custom demo

The score is an internal prioritization tool, not a prediction of conversion.

# 5. THE 5-MINUTE QUALIFICATION TEST

Before sending:

### Q1 — What happened?

Name the actual trigger.

### Q2 — What problem does it indicate?

One operational sentence.

### Q3 — Why would this matter now?

Connect the trigger to timing.

### Q4 — Who owns it?

Find the likely buyer.

### Q5 — What can Flow Vello improve?

One specific workflow.

If the team cannot answer all five, do not personalize further. Move on.

---

# 6. OUTREACH FORMULA

Do not sell the agency.

Use:

**Signal → Observation → Possible impact → Specific capability → Small CTA**

Example:

> Saw you're hiring for a sales/operations role while handling inbound inquiries through [observed channel]. That usually creates a lot of manual follow-up. We build the CRM + messaging workflow that captures, routes and follows up those leads automatically. If this is already on your roadmap, worth a 15-minute look?

The message must be based on evidence.

No:

- "We are a leading AI agency."
- "We can transform your business."
- "Would you like to increase revenue?"
- giant service lists
- fake case studies
- fake numbers

---

# 7. CHANNEL ORDER

For each high-signal account:

1. Direct work-request response, if available
2. Email
3. LinkedIn
4. Phone/WhatsApp where appropriate and legitimately available
5. Follow-up

Do not blast all channels at once.

Use the channel where the buyer originally expressed the need first.

---

# 8. FOLLOW-UP RULE

Follow-up is not:

> "Just following up."

Each follow-up adds information.

### Follow-up 1

Clarify the specific problem.

### Follow-up 2

Show the smallest relevant example/demo.

### Follow-up 3

Ask whether this is actually a priority.

### Final

Close the loop politely.

If there is no signal and no response after the sequence, stop.

Do not harass.

# 10. THE 90-MINUTE DAILY HUNT

When there is no active client delivery:

### 08:45–09:15

A finds 10–15 fresh trigger accounts.

### 09:15–09:35

A scores and filters them.

### 09:35–10:05

A sends 5 highly personalized contacts.

### 10:05–10:20

B validates technical claims for the strongest opportunities.

### 10:20–10:35

C verifies evidence and improves the reusable proof/demo.

### 10:35–11:00

A sends the remaining 5 contacts and updates the pipeline.

The objective is not 100 messages.

The objective is **10 messages to companies with evidence**.

→ **Discovery**
→ **Scoped**
→ **Proposal**

A lead without evidence is not a qualified lead.

A reply is not a qualified opportunity.

A proposal is not revenue.

A deposit is the commercial commitment.

# 14. THE ZERO-DOLLAR STACK

Use existing/free-access channels first:

- Apollo for company/contact research and available intent signals
- LinkedIn for jobs, posts, founders, and trigger verification
- Upwork and other project marketplaces for explicit active demand
- Google Search/Maps for visible business-process evidence
- company career pages for hiring triggers
- GitHub/job boards where technical hiring signals are visible
- Flow Vello demos/portfolio for proof

Do not buy another sales tool until the team proves that the current process is generating qualified conversations.

---

# 15. THE REAL ADVANTAGE

The advantage is not:

> "We are better developers."

Thousands of developers can build.

The advantage is:

> **We contact a business immediately after it gives us evidence that a specific problem exists, show that we understand the workflow, and offer a small path to fixing it.**

That is the hunting system.
